# Apple — App Store Review Guidelines & related requirements

Verified 2026-08-28. Primary source: https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/ (footer: *Last Updated: June 8, 2026*). Quotes are verbatim from that page unless another URL is given.

## Key verbatim requirements

- **1.2** — "apps with user-generated content or social networking services must include: A method for filtering objectionable material from being posted to the app; A mechanism to report offensive content and timely responses to concerns; The ability to block abusive users from the service; Published contact information so users can easily reach you." Apps used "primarily for pornographic content, Chatroulette-style experiences, random or anonymous chat, objectification of real people… making physical threats, or bullying… may be removed without notice." (2026-06) "It is your responsibility to remove content that violates this guideline… If we find such content, we will ask you to remove it, and provide a plan to improve your compliance… Egregious or repeated behavior is grounds for immediate removal."
- **1.5** — "Make sure your app and its Support URL include an easy way to contact you."
- **2.1(a)** — "placeholder text, empty websites, and other temporary content should be scrubbed before submission… include demo account info (and turn on your back-end service!) if your app includes a login… you may include a built-in demo mode in lieu of a demo account with prior approval by Apple." **2.1(b)** — IAPs must be "complete, up-to-date, visible to the reviewer and functional… explain the reason in your review notes."
- **2.3.1(a)** — "Don't include any hidden, dormant, or undocumented features… All new features, functionality, and product changes must be described with specificity in the Notes for Review section of App Store Connect (generic descriptions will be rejected)."
- **2.3.3** — "Screenshots should show the app in use, and not merely the title art, login page, or splash screen." **2.3.7** — "App names must be limited to 30 characters." **2.3.8** — metadata must "adhere to a 4+ age rating even if your app is rated higher." **2.3.10** — "don't include names, icons, or imagery of other mobile platforms or alternative app marketplaces in your app or metadata." **2.3.12** — significant changes must be listed in What's New.
- **2.4.1** — "iPhone apps should run on iPad whenever possible."
- **2.5.1** — "Apps may only use public APIs… Apps should use APIs and frameworks for their intended purposes." **2.5.2** — no downloading/executing code that changes features. **2.5.4** — "Multitasking apps may only use background services for their intended purposes: VoIP, audio playback, location, task completion, local notifications, etc." **2.5.14** — explicit consent + visual/audible indication when recording. **2.5.18** — ads only in the main binary; close/skip buttons on interstitials; users must be able to report inappropriate ads.
- **3.1.1** — "If you want to unlock features or functionality within your app… you must use in-app purchase… you should make sure you have a restore mechanism for any restorable in-app purchases." Loot-box odds must be disclosed. **3.1.1(a)** — external links allowed without entitlement only on the United States storefront; "In all other storefronts… may not include buttons, external links, or other calls to action that direct customers to purchasing mechanisms other than in-app purchase."
- **3.1.2(a)** — subscriptions "must last at least seven days and be available across all of the user's devices." **3.1.2(c)** — "clearly describe what the user will get for the price" and follow Schedule 2 (Terms of Use/EULA + privacy links in app and metadata).
- **3.1.3(e)** — physical goods/services consumed outside the app "must use purchase methods other than in-app purchase." **3.1.3(g)** — buying in-app ads/boosts must use IAP.
- **3.2.2(x)** — "Apps must not force users to rate the app, review the app, download other apps… in order to access functionality."
- **4.2** — "features, content, and UI that elevate it beyond a repackaged website." **4.2.2** — not "marketing materials, advertisements, web clippings, content aggregators, or a collection of links."
- **4.3(b)** (2026-06) — "dating, flashlight, sound effects, wallpaper, simple timers, and fortune telling, are well established… we will not accept new submissions unless they offer a meaningfully different or improved experience."
- **4.5.4** — "Push Notifications must not be required for the app to function… should not be used for promotions or direct marketing purposes unless customers have explicitly opted in… and you provide a method in your app for a user to opt out."
- **4.8** — third-party/social login for the primary account requires an equivalent option that "limits data collection to the user's name and email address; … allows users to keep their email address private…; and … does not collect interactions with your app for advertising purposes without consent." Exemptions: own account system only; alternative-marketplace login; education/enterprise accounts; government/industry eID; client for a specific third-party service.
- **5.1.1(i)** — privacy policy link "in the App Store Connect metadata field and within the app." **(ii)** — "Ensure your purpose strings clearly and completely describe your use of the data"; paid functionality must not depend on granting access. **(iii)** — prefer the out-of-process picker / share sheet over full Photos/Contacts access. **(iv)** — don't "manipulate, trick, or force people to consent"; offer alternatives (manual address when location denied). **(v)** — "If your app doesn't include significant account-based features, let people use it without a login. If your app supports account creation, you must also offer account deletion within the app… must also include a mechanism to revoke social network credentials… An app may not store credentials or tokens to social networks off of the device." **(ix)** — highly regulated fields (banking, healthcare, gambling, cannabis, air travel, crypto exchanges) must be submitted by a legal entity.
- **5.1.2(i)** — "clearly disclose where personal data will be shared with third parties, including with third-party AI, and obtain explicit permission… You must receive explicit permission from users via the App Tracking Transparency APIs to track their activity… Your app may not require users to enable system functionalities (e.g. push notifications, location services, tracking) in order to access functionality, content, use the app, or receive monetary or other compensation."
- **5.1.4** — kids apps: no third-party analytics/advertising; non-Kids apps can't imply a children audience in name/subtitle/icon/screenshots/description. **5.1.5** — location only when directly relevant; notify and obtain consent.
- **5.6.1** — "Use the provided API to prompt users to review your app… we will disallow custom review prompts." **5.6.4** — excessive negative reviews / refunds count against you.

## Apple's own list of common rejection reasons
https://developer.apple.com/distribute/app-review/ — Crashes and bugs · Broken links ("A link to user support with up-to-date contact information and a link to your privacy policy is required for all apps") · Placeholder content · Incomplete information (demo account, special configurations, demo video/hardware) · Required documentation (kids ads policy, medical clearance, third-party IP authorization, gambling/lottery/VPN licensing) · Privacy policy issues · Unclear data access requests · Inaccurate screenshots · Substandard user interface · Web clippings/content aggregators/collection of links · Repeated submission of similar apps · Copycats · Misleading users · Not enough lasting value · Submitted by incorrect entity. Stats: "90% of submissions are reviewed in less than 24 hours"; "over 40% of unresolved issues are related to guideline 2.1: App Completeness." Appeals: one per submission.
