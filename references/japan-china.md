# Japan & China — store-review gotchas with sources

Collected 2026-08-28 from Japanese (Qiita, Zenn, note, hatena, 日経xTECH, 消費者庁) and Chinese (juejin, cnblogs, zhihu, V2EX, DCloud) developer posts. Maps to checklist items JP-01 / CN-01 and the IDs listed.

## Japan

| # | Gotcha | What to do | IDs | Sources |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **MSCA / スマホ新法** (in force 2025-12-18): Apple Japan permits external purchase links / alternative payment and alternative stores; fees restructured (IAP 21%/10% commission + 5% processing ≈ 26%/15%; external link 15%/10% + PSP fee); IAP must be shown alongside; Apple disclosure sheet mandatory; external transactions reported monthly within 15 days. Google Play Japan kept 30/15% but lifted external-payment limits ("Play store listings must not mention out-of-app purchases"). Developers judge the benefit thin. | Decide per business; if enrolling, follow the disclosure/reporting rules. | JP-01, PAY-01 | https://zenn.dev/ao_4_ma/articles/e293563cf88b88 · https://xtech.nikkei.com/atcl/nxt/column/18/00001/11363/ · https://qiita.com/review_karte/items/587851ec5e0efcbdd8df · https://developer.apple.com/support/app-distribution-in-japan/ |
| 2 | **Small Business Program** 15% starts on Apple's fiscal month, not the approval date. | Plan pricing accordingly. | — | https://note.com/keepy_japan/n/na3e6ecb64d22 |
| 3 | **特定商取引法** (2022-06 amendment): subscription order screens must show price, period, auto-renewal and cancellation terms; misleading display gives consumers a cancellation right — mirrors Apple 3.1.2 / Play subscription rejections; devs link a 特商法表記 page from the paywall/support page. | Paywall shows all terms from store data; publish 特商法表記. | JP-01, PAY-02, PAY-06 | https://www.caa.go.jp/policies/policy/consumer_transaction/specified_commercial_transactions/assets/consumer_transaction_cms202_220601_05.pdf · https://it-bengosi.com/%E3%82%A2%E3%83%97%E3%83%AA%E9%96%8B%E7%99%BA%E3%81%AE%E6%B3%95%E5%BE%8B/ec-apuri/ |
| 4 | **資金決済法 / prepaid points**: purchased in-app points are 前払式支払手段; unused balance over ¥10M triggers deposit obligations unless points expire within 6 months. | Expire points ≤ 6 months or account for deposits. | JP-01 | https://topcourt-law.com/finance/six-months-prepaid-payment · https://aglaw.jp/point-shikinkessaihou/ · https://legal-leon.jp/column/621/ |
| 5 | **Play organization account for sole proprietors**: 開業届 holders can get a D-U-N-S via Apple's free lookup and register a Play organization account, skipping the 12×14 closed test. | Consider before creating a personal account. | JP-01, AND-TEST-01 | https://note.com/serene_snipe2003/n/n82e779cb0425 · https://zenn.dev/nawa_10half/articles/android-google-play-corporate-account |

## China

| # | Gotcha | What to do | IDs | Sources |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **ICP备案** for App Store China mainland (games since 2023-10; strict validation 2024-04): unfiled apps are removed; a 1.2.0 update got an app pulled, filing via Aliyun took ~6 days; the ASC app name must match the MIIT record; offline/IAP-only apps can request an exemption via Apple's contact form. | File before submitting to China mainland, or deselect the storefront. | CN-01 | https://maiyang.me/post/2026-07-14-appstore-china-icp-filing/ · https://juejin.cn/post/7351599139858628617 · https://juejin.cn/post/7392898777047760915 · https://www.v2ex.com/t/1232582 |
| 2 | **AI features → "Guideline 5 - Legal"** without a mainland licence; deselecting China mainland is the practical fix. | Deselect China or document the licence. | CN-01, AI-01 | https://qiita.com/odendayoko/items/3a3f932f7f203a7633c1 |
| 3 | **游戏版号 / 金融资质**: games need a 版号 (ICP filing for games is refused without it); loan/finance apps hit 5.2.1 / 3.2.1 without ICP + 金融许可证. Grey-hat "壳" advice is common — treat as risk. | Obtain licences or restrict storefronts. | CN-01, LAW-02 | https://juejin.cn/post/7199836397951467575 |
| 4 | **WeChat/QQ login + 4.8**: any social login requires Sign in with Apple; hiding the WeChat button when WeChat isn't installed does not exempt (an iPhone reviewer sees it); the Apple button must follow HIG; remote toggles hiding social login during review are 2.3.1 bait. | Add SIWA properly. | IOS-LOGIN-01, CONTENT-10 | https://juejin.cn/post/7196915437807042617 · https://juejin.cn/post/7363091659104124963 · https://developer.aliyun.com/article/1218213 |
| 5 | **WeChat mini-program promotion** inside an iOS app → 3.2.2. | Remove the promotion. | IOS-UI-03 | https://juejin.cn/post/7201806164391280697 |
| 6 | **Chinese SDKs on Play / App Store**: JPush needs its `google_play` jars; uni-app/DCloud runtime carries APK-install code (Play removal even without REQUEST_INSTALL_PACKAGES); Umeng code triggered Apple 2.3.1; Adjust reads installed apps (Data safety mismatch). | Use Play variants; strip install modules; declare data. | AND-PERM-13, AND-PERM-15, CONTENT-10, PRIV-02 | https://juejin.cn/post/7115298654214324255 · https://juejin.cn/post/7249000144092020792 · https://juejin.cn/post/7485507817186279465 · https://zenn.dev/corrupt/articles/b5acb62a1ef915 |
| 7 | **Overseas-IP blocking by ops** (运维禁用了海外IP) is a recurring 2.1 cause; Play reviewers also come from non-local regions (Manila seen from Japan). | Open reviewer regions + IPv6 during review. | CONTENT-09 | https://juejin.cn/post/7116301510887079967 · https://zenn.dev/mtatsum/articles/19b077328fd305 |
| 8 | **Play account bans for new Chinese accounts** ("高风险", 账号关联): appeals occasionally succeed; guides promoting "anti-association" VPS and account purchasing describe policy violations, not fixes. | Clean account setup; never buy accounts. | AND-ENFORCE-01 | https://juejin.cn/post/7449362679247241242 · https://juejin.cn/post/7379509948902768691 |
| 9 | **MIIT requires CallKit inactive** in China; "OpenAI" in metadata affects China availability. | Disable CallKit for the CN storefront; check metadata. | IOS-ENT-04, META-02 | see Apple 2nd-pass cases in `../rejections/community-cases.md` |
