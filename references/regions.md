# Regional distribution gotchas — Russia/Belarus, Brazil, Vietnam, Indonesia, Turkey, Germany, France

Collected 2026-08-28 from developer forums, legal blogs and press in each language (Habr, vc.ru, comunidade.fabapp.com, r10.net, android-hilfe.de, viblo, Kompas, Les Numériques…). Maps to checklist item **LAW-04**; Korea → `korea.md`, Japan/China → `japan-china.md`. Items marked as evasion advice in the source communities (relatives' IDs, VDS/IP changes, feature-flagged storefronts) are policy violations and are listed only as risks.

**Russia / Belarus (ru)**
- Sanctions blocks: Belarus-registered Apple developer accounts were deactivated in Feb 2022 with the notice "Информация о юридическом лице… соответствует стране с ограниченным доступом или… санкционного списка"; funds frozen, apps hidden as "недоступно в Вашей стране" — later partially reversed. [habr 649887](https://habr.com/ru/articles/649887/). From Feb 2024/2025 Apple blocks Russian fintech apps *and* closed ADEP (enterprise) for Russian companies; banks respond with "clone" apps (feature-flagged storefront during review, obfuscated strings, Georgian dev accounts) — a 2.3.1 hidden-feature violation by design. [habr 898674](https://habr.com/ru/articles/898674/).
- Payments: Apple Pay and direct card payments in the Russian App Store were switched off in March 2022; carrier billing then broke (МегаФон removed), Qiwi top-ups stopped. [habr 665304](https://habr.com/ru/articles/665304/). Developer fee: ~8,990 ₽/yr payable only with foreign cards or an МТС card; "Российские счета Apple сейчас не принимает". [vc.ru 941078](https://vc.ru/marketing/941078-kak-vypustit-prilozhenie-v-app-store-esli-ty-v-rossii). Payouts require a real-bank account in a non-sanctioned country; virtual/crypto cards rejected; Turkish card failed validation, Serbian business account accepted. [habr 1015122](https://habr.com/ru/articles/1015122/).
- Play Console: new accounts cannot be registered with RF profile data (2022 Q&A and 2025 experience); workaround is a relative's identity + foreign residence card + physical Android device login. [qna 1199686](https://qna.habr.com/q/1199686), [habr 1015122](https://habr.com/ru/articles/1015122/). Apple identity check in Georgia rejects a Russian foreign passport ("документ не принимается для выбранной страны") — Georgian ID/residence permit needed. [qna 1411960](https://qna.habr.com/q/1411960).
- Removals on Roskomnadzor demand: 25 VPN apps removed from the Russian App Store in July 2024 (Red Shield, Le VPN, Proton, NordVPN…), ~98 by Dec 2024; RKN fines 0.8–4 M ₽ for not removing "VPN popularisation" content. [habr 826694](https://habr.com/ru/articles/826694/), [habr 869850](https://habr.com/ru/articles/869850/). Russian court ordered Google to restore sports.ru's Play Console (100k ₽/day penalty) — enforceable only if Google returns. [habr 908596](https://habr.com/ru/articles/908596/).
- Off-Play distribution (RuStore/APK) loses the pre-release policy dialogue: 2ГИС could not appeal a Play Protect "spyware" flag because it is not on Play. [vc.ru 986803](https://vc.ru/services/986803-vsya-istoriya-s-google-play-i-2gis-nash-post-mortem).

**Brazil (pt-BR)**
- ECA Digital (Lei 15.211/2025, in force 17 Mar 2026; decrees 12.622/2025 and 12.880/2026): mandatory age assurance (self-declaration no longer allowed), parental linkage for under-16 social accounts, ban on infinite scroll/autoplay/emotional notifications for minors, **loot boxes banned in games accessible to minors**, no profiling/ads on minors. [Agência Lupa](https://www.agencialupa.org/jornalismo/2026/03/18/eca-digital-o-que-muda-na-pratica/), [Senado](https://www12.senado.leg.br/radio/1/noticia/2026/03/27/eca-digital-proibe-rolagem-infinita-e-caixa-de-recompensa-em-games-infantojuvenis). ANPD decided (Jun 2026) that OS vendors and app stores (Play Store/App Store) are the first line of age-verification enforcement; the Justice Ministry had already notified Google/Apple over betting apps reachable by minors (Apr 2026). [Olhar Digital](https://olhardigital.com.br/2026/06/19/internet-e-redes-sociais/play-store-e-app-store-brasil-impoe-mudancas-nas-lojas-de-aplicativos/). Apple's Feb 2026 age-assurance update blocks 18+ downloads without confirmed age in Brazil (with Australia, Singapore) and exposes new Declared Age Range signals. [economiematin](https://www.economiematin.fr/protection-mineurs-apple-controle-age-app-store).
- Payments/stores: after the CADE settlement (Dec 2025), Apple allows alternative app stores and alternative payment methods (incl. redirect to external checkout/PIX) in Brazil; IAP keeps a 5 % processing fee on top of up to 21 % commission; alternative stores still need Apple authorization. [TecMundo](https://www.tecmundo.com.br/mercado/413975-apple-oficializa-lojas-externas-no-brasil-e-outros-meios-de-pagamento-na-app-store.htm), [Folha](https://www1.folha.uol.com.br/mercado/2025/12/apple-permitira-baixar-apps-fora-da-app-store-e-pagamento-via-pix-apos-acordo-com-cade.shtml).
- Developer identity: Play Console requires CPF/CNPJ-matching identity and (for orgs) D-U-N-S; the no-code community sees repeated "Verificação de contas" waves (Jan 2024) with account loss for non-completion. [Fabapp 6520](https://comunidade.fabapp.com/t/verificacao-de-contas-google-play-console/6520), [Play Console help pt-BR](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/13628312?hl=pt-br). Municipal/civic apps are frequently hit by Families (target audience) and Government-services/Misleading-claims policies. [Fabapp 4448](https://comunidade.fabapp.com/t/resolvido-app-suspenso-pela-google-play-motivo-violacao-a-politica-de-familia/4448), [Fabapp 6196](https://comunidade.fabapp.com/t/problemas-com-atualizacao-do-app-na-play-store/6196).

**Vietnam (vi)**
- Decree 147/2024/NĐ-CP (9 Nov 2024): app stores must remove games lacking a Vietnamese G1 release decision (G2–G4: notification confirmation); foreign stores that do not comply face technical blocking by MIC/MPS. Only Vietnam-registered enterprises may publish online games, so foreign studios go through a local publisher. [VietNamNet](https://vietnamnet.vn/se-chan-duoc-game-khong-phep-game-bai-phat-hanh-qua-google-va-apple-2345855.html), [Gamota](https://gamota.com/gamota-lab/moblie-game-publishing-license-regulations-in-vietnam/). Apple/Google both require the local game licence in the listing for Vietnam.
- Fintech/pawn/lending apps: App Review asks for the business licence and Vietnam-only availability. [BigDataTech](https://bigdatatech.vn/case-study/kinh-nghiem-submit-app-len-apple-store-va-nhung-loi-de-bi-reject).

**Indonesia (id)**
- PSE Lingkup Privat registration with Kominfo (now Komdigi): deadline 20 July 2022; unregistered platforms were treated as illegal and blocked (Epic Games/Unreal launcher blocked Aug 2022, disrupting local studios). [Kompas](https://tekno.kompas.com/read/2022/07/19/09000057/beberapa-game-sudah-terdaftar-di-halaman-pse-ke-kominfo-ada-mobile-legends-dan), [detikINET](https://inet.detik.com/games-news/d-6223942/epic-games-diblokir-kominfo-bikin-developer-game-lokal-sulit-berkarya).
- Local white-label shops selling game top-ups via bank transfer are systematically rejected for "Violation of Payment Policy"; vendor guidance encourages hiding products during review (risk of CONTENT-10 style enforcement). [bukaOlshop](https://help.bukaolshop.com/tips-tips-mengatasi-upload-ke-playstore-ditolak-reject/).

**Turkey (tr)**
- KVKK: the Personal Data Protection Authority's Dec 2023 "Mobil Uygulamalarda Mahremiyetin Korunmasına Yönelik Tavsiyeler" guide expects in-app aydınlatma (notice) and consent modules, age checks and child-readable notices in apps children may use, and data-processor agreements with cloud providers. [Ozay Law summary](https://ozay.av.tr/publication/mobil-uygulama-rehberi-isiginda-mobil-uygulamalarda-kvk-uyumu).
- Play Store age verification (ID upload, selfie, card or verifymy.io) rolled out in Turkey from Oct 2025; unverified users cannot download 18+ apps. [DonanımHaber](https://www.donanimhaber.com/google-play-store-a-yas-dogrulama-sistemi-geliyor--197870).
- Account terminations for "association" are common (purchased reskins from forums, self-made tester accounts); local forums openly trade evasion advice (relatives' IDs, VDS/IP changes) which Google's policy explicitly bans; deleted payments profiles cannot be recreated; Google's local entity (Google Reklamcılık ve Pazarlama Ltd. Şti.) is the defendant for Turkish lawsuits. [r10 3440380](https://www.r10.net/sorum-var/3440380-play-console-hesabim-kapatildi-ne-yapmam-gerek.html), [r10 3965861](https://www.r10.net/android-ios-uygulama-gelistirme/3965861-kapatilan-gelistirici-hesabi-nasil-tekrar-acilir.html), [donanimhaber](https://forum.donanimhaber.com/play-console-hesabim-haksiz-yere-kapatildi-ne-yapabilirim--158802406), [Legalter](https://www.legalter.net/google-play-store-console-gelistirici-developer-hesabim-kapatildi-ne-yapabilirim-turkiyede-dava-acabilir-miyim/).

**Germany (de)**
- Impressum duty (TMG/DDG §5) applies to apps, not only websites — the Impressum must be reachable inside the app and in the store listing; missing/incorrect Impressum and Datenschutzerklärung are classic Abmahnung targets. [eRecht24](https://www.e-recht24.de/impressum/10176-app-impressum.html), [IT-Recht Kanzlei](https://www.it-recht-kanzlei.de/datenschutzerklaerung-fuer-app.html).
- Play's public-contact/verification rule collides with German privacy expectations: solo devs who ever monetised must publish a physical address — Authenticator Pro left Play for F-Droid. [android-hilfe](https://www.android-hilfe.de/forum/tools.441/authenticator-pro-aus-dem-play-store-verbannt.1100961.html). DSGVO consent as legal basis is taken literally: a village app removed the Firebase token until notifications are allowed (Aug 2026). [roessing PR](https://github.com/dorfentwicklungskreis-roessing/app/pull/3).
- Official-sounding names/government data: Play's Impersonation and Misleading-claims policies require "(inoffiziell)" in the title and linked official sources. [sgt-rechner](https://github.com/MarqEwi/sgt-rechner/pull/13).

**France (fr)**
- Age verification: France legislated a social-media ban under 15 with age verification for all (Jul 2026, after the SREN-era ARCOM porn-site regime); platforms push Apple/Google to verify at the store level, which both refuse; ARCOM supervises. [Les Numériques](https://www.lesnumeriques.com/societe-numerique/la-france-impose-la-verification-d-age-pour-tous-en-bannissant-les-reseaux-sociaux-pour-les-moins-de-15-ans-n259571.html), [France 24](https://www.france24.com/fr/info-en-continu/20260124-v%C3%A9rification-de-l-%C3%A2ge-des-internautes-les-g%C3%A9ants-de-la-tech-se-renvoient-la-balle). Apple's Feb 2026 global age-assurance update (Declared Age Range API) is the mechanism developers must adopt. [economiematin](https://www.economiematin.fr/protection-mineurs-apple-controle-age-app-store).
- Google Play account terminations: French counsel note Google's 180-day appeal window for terminations since 28 Jan 2026 and the "associated accounts" logic; SMEs lose accounts when the notified e-mail belongs to a departed employee (verification deadline missed). [Pouget Avocat](https://pouget-avocat.fr/portfolio/avocat-pour-compte-developpeur-google-suspendu/), [mgmobile](https://www.mgmobile.fr/nos-actualites-application-5-etoiles-2-1/mon-compte-google-play-store-est-bloque-et-mon-appli-est-indisponible-131).
